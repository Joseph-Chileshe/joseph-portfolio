1. CBD Parking Management System

The CBD Parking Management System is a comprehensive, full stack digital platform designed for both drivers and municipal parking officers. It serves as a complete operational ecosystem that handles automated reporting, both offline and online digital payments, and live revenue monitoring in one unified system.

This system was built to solve the problem of inefficient manual parking management. It eliminates physical paper receipts, reduces the heavy workload on parking officers who manually issue them, and removes the reliance on guesswork by providing a reliable way to instantly verify if a parked vehicle has paid, along with the approximate parking period. By automating these simultaneous, fast paced daily operations, the platform gives administrators live oversight of parking activity and revenue in the city in real time.

Key Features

  • Instant Vehicle Payment Verification: Officers can instantly check a license plate or receipt to confirm if a vehicle has paid and its exact parking duration.
  • Driver-Officer Verification System: A built-in two-way trust system where drivers can also verify if an officer is a genuine, authorized city official.
  • Vehicle Flagging & Enforcement Support: Automatically flags notorious vehicles with a history of over-parking or multiple reports, alerting officers to prioritize them for inspection.
  • Real-Time Revenue Monitoring: Live dashboards for administrators to track city revenue instantly as it is collected.
  • Automated Reporting & Audit Trails: Instant generation of daily transaction reports and secure digital audit trails for complete financial transparency.
  • Electronic Receipts & Digital Records: Replaces physical paper with instant digital receipts, reducing waste and eliminating lost records.
  • Flexible Integration with Existing Council Systems: Built as a standalone architecture that can seamlessly complement or integrate with other municipal solutions without requiring a complete overhaul.
  • Digital & Offline Access (Smartphone, SMS, USSD): Provides flexible payment options, including an SMS/USSD fallback for drivers without smartphones or internet access.

Deployment Requirements

For the system to operate at maximum efficiency, the following is required:

  • Parking Area Labelling: Each parking zone must have clearly visible signs displaying the area code (e.g., LMB01 for Lumumba Parking Zone 1), a scannable QR code that links directly to the web portal, and an SMS number for those without a smartphone.
  • Any Simple Smartphone: Parking officers will need smartphones or tablets with the Warden App installed, loaded with data packages.
  • Admin Dashboard Access: The City Council requires a computer or tablet in their control room to access the live Admin Dashboard and monitor the city's parking activity.
  • Telecom Integration: Registration with mobile network operators (Airtel, MTN, Zamtel) to support the SMS/USSD fallback and mobile money processing.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: Flutter
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python)
  • Database: PostgreSQL
