1. CBD Parking Management System

The CBD Parking Management System is a comprehensive, full-stack digital platform designed for both drivers and municipal parking officers. It serves as a complete operational ecosystem that handles automated reporting, both offline and online digital payments, and live revenue monitoring in one unified system.

This system was built to solve the problem of inefficient manual parking management. It eliminates physical paper receipts, reduces the heavy workload on parking officers who manually issue them, and removes the reliance on guesswork by providing a reliable way to instantly verify if a parked vehicle has paid, along with the approximate parking period. By automating these simultaneous, fast-paced daily operations, the platform gives administrators live oversight of revenue and parking activity in the city in real time.

Key Features

  • Digital & Offline Payments: Secure mobile and cash payment processing, plus an SMS/USSD fallback for drivers without smartphones or internet access.
  • Live Revenue Monitoring: Real-time dashboards for administrators to track city revenue instantly.
  • Automated Reporting: Instant generation of daily transaction reports and digital audit trails.
  • Dual Verification System: A built-in two-way trust system where officers can verify if a vehicle has paid via its license plate or receipt, and drivers can verify if an officer is a genuine, authorized city official.
  • Vehicle Flagging System: Automatically flags notorious vehicles with a history of over-parking or multiple reports, alerting officers to prioritize them for inspection.

Flexible Integration: Built as a standalone system, its architecture allows it to seamlessly complement other existing solutions to enhance parking operations efficiency. It is designed to add value without requiring a complete system overhaul.

Deployment Requirements

For the system to operate at maximum efficiency, the following is required:

  • Parking Area Labelling: Each parking zone must have clearly visible signs displaying the area code (e.g., LMB01 for Lumumba Parking Zone 1), a scannable QR code that links directly to the web portal, and an SMS number for those without a smartphone.
  • Any Simple Smartphone: Parking officers will need smartphones or tablets with the Warden App installed, loaded with data packages.
  • Admin Dashboard Access: The City Council requires a computer or tablet in their control room to access the live Admin Dashboard and monitor the city's parking activity.
  • Telecom Integration: Registration with mobile network operators (Airtel, MTN, Zamtel) to support the SMS/USSD fallback and mobile money processing.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: Flutter
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python)
  • Database: PostgreSQL
