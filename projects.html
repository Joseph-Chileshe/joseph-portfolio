1. CBD Parking Management System

The CBD Parking Management System is a full-stack digital platform designed for drivers and municipal parking officers. It streamlines the entire parking process by handling digital payments, live revenue monitoring, and automated reporting.

Built to solve real-world challenges in Zambia, the system was designed to reduce payment leakage, increase parking efficiency, and simplify daily parking operations that happen simultaneously for officers on the ground.

Project Demo

Key Features

  • Digital Payments: Secure mobile and cash payment processing.
  • Live Revenue Monitoring: Real-time dashboards for administrators to track city revenue.
  • Automated Reporting: Instant generation of daily transaction reports and digital audit trails.
  • Dual Verification System: A built-in two-way trust system where officers can verify if a vehicle has paid via its license plate or receipt, and drivers can verify if an officer is a genuine, authorized city official.

Flexible Integration: Built as a standalone solution, its architecture allows it to seamlessly complement other existing solutions to enhance parking operations efficiency. It is designed to add value without requiring a complete system overhaul.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: Flutter
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python)
  • Database: PostgreSQL
